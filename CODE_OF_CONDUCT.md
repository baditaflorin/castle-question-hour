# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), v2.1.

## In short

Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith. Don't tolerate harassment, doxxing,
or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report issues to **baditaflorin@gmail.com**. Reports are confidential. Maintainers may warn, mute,
or ban contributors who violate this code.
